# LAST STANCE: Development Notes

**A 1v1 hand-to-hand fighting game built in Unity 6.6 (URP): motion-captured martial artists fighting in a sunset temple courtyard, with hit-stop, blood, parries, throws, juggles and slow-motion knockouts.**

> Kaito "The Blade Hand" (fast striker) vs Brakka "The Iron Fist" (heavy brute).
> 1 player vs AI · best of 3 rounds · keyboard or gamepad · Windows build.

## Quick start

| What | Command |
|---|---|
| Play the game | `Build\LastStance.exe` |
| Full rebuild (scene + player) | `powershell -File tools\build.ps1` |
| Rebuild player only | `powershell -File tools\build.ps1 -SkipScene` |

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move / sidestep (screen-relative) | `W A S D` / arrows | Left stick / D-pad |
| Light attack | `J` (or `Z`) | X / □ |
| Heavy attack | `K` (or `X`) | Y / △ |
| Launcher (Rising Knee) | toward opponent + `K` | toward + Y |
| Block (hold) · **Parry** (tap just before the hit lands) | `L` (or `C`) | LB / LT |
| Dodge / dash (i-frames) | `Shift` / `Space` | A / RB |
| Throw (beats block) · throw break | `U` or `J+K` | B |
| Pause · quit from pause | `Esc` · `Q` | Start |
| Rematch | `Enter` | Start |

**Strings:** `J J J J` jab → cross → hook → Rising Dragon launcher · `J J K` jab → cross → spinning back kick · `K K` roundhouse → spinning back kick · `J J J K` ends in the high kick knockdown · after a launcher, `J J J` juggles.

## Game systems

- **Combat:** 9 moves in branching strings; hit windows derived from each clip's analysed impact frame; strikes are tested against the real bone positions (fist knuckles, ball of the foot, knee) and a hurt capsule from hips to head.
- **Defence:** hold block (chip damage on heavies, stamina drain, guard break at zero stamina); **parry** = block pressed within 0.12 s of impact → attacker staggered, slow-mo, +25% punish damage; **dodge** has i-frames and triggers "EVADE!" slow-mo when dashing through a strike.
- **Stamina:** attacks, dodges, throws and blocked hits cost stamina; exhausted fighters attack slower and cannot dash.
- **Air game:** launchers, juggles (up to 5 hits, decaying pop height), knockdowns, get-up with invincibility.
- **Throws:** grab-and-slam (Brute's mocap) that beats blocking; throw break by pressing throw back quickly.
- **Feel:** hit-stop (frame freeze), camera shake and FOV punch, blood splashes that collide with the floor, fading stains, sparks, shockwave rings, chromatic-aberration pulse on heavies, hit flash on the body.
- **AI:** plans (approach, hover, circle, retreat, attack), reads each incoming attack with a skill-scaled human reaction delay and chooses block/parry/dodge/take, keeps guard up through strings, punishes whiffs and parries, throws turtling players, breaks throws, backs off to recover stamina, never attacks a downed or rising opponent.
- **Presentation:** crane-shot round intro with gong, "FIGHT!", combo counter with D→SSS style rank, move-name callouts, "PERFECT K.O.", victory orbit, taiko battle music.

## Animation technology (the console-grade stack)

Each frame the layers run in this order:

```
Mocap clip (Playables, humanoid retarget, per-clip foot IK)
   ↓  AnimDriver.LateUpdate     – inertialization: decaying per-bone offset from the previous pose
   ↓  Fighter.LateUpdate        – floor clamp for lying / falling bodies
   ↓  HitSpring.LateUpdate      – procedural spring hit reaction on spine / neck / head
   ↓  FootLock.LateUpdate       – foot locking + two-bone leg IK, floor constraint
Final pose
```

| Technique | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Motion-captured animation (Mixamo) + humanoid retargeting** | Realistic motion; one clip set drives both body types | `MixamoImport.cs`, `AnimDriver.cs` |
| **Automatic clip analysis** | Hit windows, striking limb and reach come from the mocap data, so hits land exactly when the fist or foot is extended | `AnimAnalyzer.cs` |
| **Inertialization** (Gears of War / The Last of Us II technique) | Crossfades average two poses and make bodies sag and bounce. Inertialization switches instantly and decays the leftover pose offset with a quintic curve, so motion keeps its momentum and never snaps | `AnimDriver.cs` |
| **Procedural additive hit reactions** (Tekken / Street Fighter style) | Light and medium hits no longer swap clips: a critically-damped spring bends the upper body away from the exact impact point while the guard animation keeps playing. Heavy blows still play a full reaction clip | `HitSpring.cs` |
| **Root-motion hygiene** | Ignore the bogus root-motion jump on a clip's first frame and cap root motion at lunge speed (fixed the "bounce back after every hit") | `Fighter.OnAnimatorMove` |
| **Foot locking + analytic two-bone IK** (Daniel Holden's method) | A toe that is low and slow is locked to the floor; hip and knee are re-solved (cosine rule) so the planted foot stays put. Locks blend in and out, the knee keeps the animation's bend plane, extension is soft-clamped, and feet never go below the floor | `FootLock.cs` |
| **Per-clip foot IK** | Unity's humanoid foot IK stops retargeted knees twisting on standing clips, but it fights the body on the floor, so it is off for falling / lying / get-up clips | `AnimDriver.cs` |
| **Floor clamp** | Lifts a lying body if any bone would sink into the stone | `Fighter.LateUpdate` |
| **Body-space camera** | Frames the hips, not the root transforms, so the camera always follows what the player sees | `FightCamera.cs` |
| **Hit-stop on scaled time** | Everything (clips, springs, particles) freezes together during the impact freeze | `FX.cs` (`TimeFX`) |

## Tools and pipelines built for this project

- **`tools/build.ps1`:** one command rebuilds the scene and the player, runs an AI-vs-AI test match with screenshots, and launches the game. It retries automatically and **self-heals stale Unity build caches** (`Library/Bee`, `Library/BuildPlayerData`) left by hung builds.
- **`SceneBuilder`:** the whole arena is generated from code, so it is reproducible.
- **`AnimAnalyzer`:** frame-by-frame mocap analysis plus a contact-sheet renderer to check every clip visually.
- **`AutoCapture` test harness:** screenshots of intros, heavy hits (captured during hit-stop), parries, throws, knockdowns, get-ups, K.O.s and victory. It logs every hit with the body gap and limb distance, and runs a **per-frame motion monitor** that counts hip snaps, foot skating and backward-bending knees.
- **Pose sheet / contact sheet renderers:** visual QA of every pose or clip without playing the game.
- **Blender pipeline:** procedural GLB generation for the arena, run headless (`blender -b -P`).
- **Mixamo batch pipeline:** exported through Mixamo's own API inside the user's logged-in browser tab, one fresh tab per file (see problems below).
- **Asset fetch script:** Poly Haven API downloader for HDRI, textures and props.

## Problems we hit and how they were fixed

| # | Problem | Root cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Characters glowed white, washed-out skin | Too much sun + post exposure + bloom; over-strong hit flash | Re-balanced lighting and tonemapping; flash is now a short red tint |
| 2 | Player build failed after an interrupted session | Stale `Library/Bee` code-gen cache | Delete the cache; `build.ps1` now does it automatically before retries |
| 3 | Crash on every parry | Attack state read after the parry cleared the move | Return right after the hit check if the state changed |
| 4 | AI parried almost everything | It pressed guard the instant an attack started | Human-like, skill-scaled reaction delay; keeps guard through strings |
| 5 | Juggles never connected | Launches sent bodies too high | Lower launch arcs, floatier juggle gravity, hurt capsule includes the legs while airborne |
| 6 | Mixamo downloads silently vanished | Chrome allows one download per tab here, and the signed links expire fast | Export and save each file from a brand-new tab (API export → `location.assign`) |
| 7 | Brute rendered pink with a stray shape overhead | Lowercase `_diffuse` names made the colour map get re-imported as a normal map; the model ships holding an axe | Keyword-based texture matching that never reuses the colour map; the axe and moustache meshes are hidden |
| 8 | Clip analysis returned impact 0 for everything | Sampling ran without a scene, on a prefab instance | New scene, unpacked instance, `Rebind`, `Evaluate(0)` |
| 9 | Paired throw had the victim 4 m away; fights looked "in different places" | Mixamo's paired clips carry baked root offsets | Replaced with a grab-and-slam where both bodies stay on their own roots; the camera frames the hips |
| 10 | Bodies sank into the floor after throws / knockdowns | Clip poses dip below the floor after retargeting | Floor clamp in `LateUpdate` (first version had a runaway lift bug, fixed) |
| 11 | Legs bent strangely while getting up | Foot IK pulled the legs toward the floor; long get-up clip with a slow blend | Foot IK off for lying / rising clips; get-up trimmed to the analysed "upright" frame and played at 2.4× |
| 12 | Fighter kept attacking a downed opponent | AI didn't treat down / rising as a pause | AI steps back and waits in guard |
| 13 | Health dropped without visible contact | Hit radii too generous for realistic bodies | Tighter limb radii and body capsules; every hit logs its body gap |
| 14 | Actions and reactions too slow | Mocap at natural speed with long wind-ups | Attacks at about 1.6× with trimmed wind-ups; hit reactions at 1.8×; shorter hitstun |
| 15 | **"Characters bounce back after every hit"** | Starting a clip mid-way made Unity report the whole skipped root motion as one frame's movement; plus clip-swap hit reactions and crossfades | Ignore root motion on a clip's first frame, clamp to lunge speed, inertialization, spring hit reactions. **Measured snaps: 114 → 11 per match** |
| 16 | Legs deform during attacks, feet skate | Foot IK had been disabled globally; no foot planting | Per-clip foot IK plus the FootLock two-bone IK system |
| 17 | Grabbed fighter snapped into the throw hold | Teleport to the hold position | Eased in over about 0.1 s |
| 18 | Unity player build hung in `bee_backend` | Known intermittent Unity hang on this PC | Kill and rebuild; `build.ps1` cleans caches before retrying |

## Testing and metrics

Every build can run `-autocapture`, which plays a full AI-vs-AI best of 3 and logs:

- hits, heavies, knockdowns, max combo, juggles, blocks, parries, guard breaks, evades, grabs, throws, throw breaks
- `maxGap`: the largest body-to-body distance at which any hit registered (sanity check against damage without contact)
- `snaps` / `worstHipSpeed`: frames where a body's hips jumped faster than 9 m/s (pops, teleports, bounces)
- `skatePerMin`: how far planted toes slid along the floor per standing minute
- `kneeFlipFrames`: frames where a knee bent backwards

Before and after the root-motion fix: **114 → 11 snaps per match**. Of the 11, 3 came from the throw hold, since fixed by the eased grab; the rest are the fast motion of the heavy-reaction clip itself. Matches run at 120 fps.
