# LAST STANCE

### *Two fighters. One courtyard. No weapons. Only one walks away.*

**Last Stance** is a brutal 1v1 hand-to-hand fighting game. Two martial artists meet in an ancient temple courtyard at sunset: vermilion pillars, glowing stone lanterns, burning braziers and cherry blossoms drifting across the stones. Every punch, kick and knee is **real motion capture**, every impact freezes time for a split second, and every knockout plays out in slow motion.

A lean, precise striker who wins with speed. Kaito chains fast jabs into long combos, slips in and out of range, and punishes every mistake.

## How to Play

**Launch:** double-click `Build\LastStance.exe`

**Match:** best of 3 rounds, 60 seconds each. Empty your opponent's health bar for a **K.O.** Win a round without taking damage for a **PERFECT K.O.** If time runs out, the fighter with more health wins.

### Controls

| Action | Keyboard + mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move / sidestep | Arrow keys (`W A S D` also work) | Left stick / D-pad |
| Look around (boss fight) | Move the mouse · wheel zooms | |
| Turn to face Moros | Middle click | |
| Light attack | Left click (or `J`) | X / □ |
| Heavy attack | Right click (or `K`) | Y / △ |
| Launcher (Rising Knee) | Toward opponent + right click | Toward + Y |
| Block (hold) | Right `Ctrl` or mouse back button (or `L`) | LB / LT |
| **Parry** | Tap block just before a hit lands | Tap LB just before a hit lands |
| Dodge / dash | `Shift` or mouse forward button | RB |
| **Wall run / leap** (boss fight) | `Space`: run up the wall in front of you onto the roof, or leap (across the rooftops) | A |
| **Hero powers** (boss fight) | `1` Thunder Palm · `2` Guardian Spirit · `3` Blade Hand Awakening · `4` Stone Gauntlets (also numpad `1`–`4`, or `Delete` / `End` / `Page Down` / `Insert` above the arrows) | hold RT + X / Y / B / A |
| Throw | Left + right click together, or `Enter` (or `U`) | B |
| Break a throw | Press throw right after being grabbed | B |
| Pause · quit from pause | `Esc` · `Q` | Start |
| Rematch | `Enter` | Start |

Movement follows the screen: push right to move right on screen. The mouse cursor is hidden while you play and comes back when you pause. In a normal match `Space` is a second dodge key. Up and down sidestep around your opponent, Tekken-style. When the camera cuts to a new angle, your current direction is kept until you let go of the stick. In the final boss fight, move away from Moros and Kaito breaks into a sprint through the temple town. Move the mouse and the camera is yours: it orbits Kaito and the arrow keys follow where you look. Leave the mouse alone for a few seconds and the automatic camera takes back over. An orange marker always shows where Moros is, with his distance; when he is off screen it sits on the edge and points to him. Red edge markers show hunting swarms that are on your trail.

### Combos to try

- `J J J J` → **4-hit string** ending in a launcher, then `J J J` to **juggle** them in the air
- `J J K` → jab, cross, **Spinning Back Kick** knockdown
- Toward + `K` → Rising Knee launch → `J J J` air combo
- **Parry** a heavy attack → the attacker is left wide open → any combo (+25% damage)

### Brakka's new AI pressure routes

Brakka now uses three extra motion-captured close-range attacks, giving his pressure a different rhythm to Kaito's fast strings: `J K` → **Elbow Crush**, `J J J` → **Iron Hook**, and `J J J K` → **Skullbreaker** headbutt knockdown. These are used by the AI, so repeated matches have more variety.

## Combat Mechanics

- **Hit-stop:** time freezes for a split second on every impact, so hits feel heavy.
- **Blocking:** holding block stops light hits completely. Heavy hits still chip through, and every blocked hit drains stamina.
- **Guard break:** block with no stamina left and your guard shatters, leaving you open.
- **Parry:** tap block at the last moment. The attacker staggers, time slows, and **PARRY!** flashes on screen.
- **Evade:** dodge through an attack at the last instant for **EVADE!** slow motion.
- **Stamina:** the thin bar under your health. Attacks, dodges and throws cost stamina, and it refills when you ease off. Run out and you attack slower and can't dodge.
- **Juggles:** launched opponents can be hit again in mid-air, up to 5 air hits.
- **Knockdowns:** big hits drop your opponent. They are invincible while getting up; there's no hitting a fallen fighter.
- **Throws:** a throw beats a blocking opponent. The victim can break it by reacting fast.

### Style Rank

Keep hitting without letting your opponent recover to climb the combo ranks:

`D` → `C` → `B` → `A` → `S` → `SS` → **`SSS`**

## The Arena: Temple Courtyard at Sunset

A walled temple courtyard at golden hour:

- A vermilion main hall and a five-tier pagoda silhouetted against the sunset
- A towering torii gate
- Stone lanterns and flickering fire braziers casting warm light
- Cherry blossom trees with petals drifting through the fight
- Weathered flagstone floor that keeps the marks of battle

Beyond the courtyard walls, open gates lead into the **temple town**, where the final boss fight spills out:

- **East market street:** two-storey townhouses with lattice fronts, market stalls and a well, with narrow alleys and a back lane between the houses
- **West storehouse quarter:** white-walled storehouses, a bell tower in the plaza and stacked crates
- **South shrine approach:** a lantern-lined avenue past a tea house, a shrine office and storehouses, ending at a second torii
- **Temple heights** (north, behind the main hall): the pagoda plaza, a bell tower, a rock garden and the **Grand Dojo**, a long training hall you can enter, with red pillars, tatami, weapon racks and an altar
- **Brewery quarter** (far east, through the east gate): the **Sake Brewery**, a timber warehouse full of giant vats and barrel stacks to hide behind
- **Bamboo quarter** (far west, through the west gate): the **Tea House**, a ryokan of paper-walled rooms off a central corridor, among bamboo groves

Almost every roof in town can be climbed: houses, storehouses, the bell tower, the dojo, the brewery and the tea house, plus wall tops, stall canopies and crate stacks.

## Final Boss: MOROS, THE SHARD MONK

Beat Brakka and the courtyard floor tears itself apart. A pale stone monk with burning eyes rises from a tornado of shattered flagstones, and every stone in the temple obeys him. His swarm of thousands of stone shards **assembles into weapons**, strikes, and shatters back into shards:

| Weapon | What it does | How to survive |
|---|---|---|
| **Executioner's Axe** | A giant war axe chops down a red line; below half health it follows with a full sweep | Sidestep the chop or tap block to parry; dash through the sweep |
| **Titan Grasp** | A giant stone hand on a stone arm lunges, catches Kaito, crushes him and slams him | Dash or sidestep, or punch the hand to break it |
| **Lance Storm** | A fan of stone spears fires one after another and sticks in the ground | Keep moving, parry the lances |
| **Serpent Dragon** | A shard serpent with a dragon's head coils and lunges | Dash through it, or punch its head to shatter it |
| **Stone Roc** | The bird, rebuilt from stone, circles the temple and dives | Dash as it dives |
| **Skybreaker** | A colossal hammer tracks you from the sky and smashes a red ring | Get out of the ring |
| **Shard Flood** | A breaking wave of stone rolls across the floor | Dash through the wave |

**The Titan.** Low on health, Moros ascends: every shard in the courtyard builds a **10 m armoured stone titan** around him, rising from the boots up, and it comes after you while it is still building, stone legs striding out of a storm of shards that is still becoming its body. You keep full control the whole time. It has plated legs, a runed belt, a harnessed red chest plate, layered pauldrons, iron gauntlets and a bearded stone head with burning eyes. It walks after you, slams the ground and stomps out shockwaves, and it can pick you up to its face, roar, and hurl you across the temple. Then it crumbles and leaves Moros open.

**The Hunt.** Run far enough and Moros rises over the rooftops and sends **hunting swarms** through the streets. The stone serpents roam the town looking for you, but they only see you in a clear line of sight, so break away down an alley, duck behind a house or hide under the trees. If one spots you, it chases; if it reaches you, it closes into a hand and drags you back to Moros. Dash through a lunging hunter, or punch its head to shatter it.

**Punish him.** After his powers, Moros lands and swings at you. When **HE'S OPEN!** appears, that is your moment.

### The villain's arc

| Phase | What changes |
|---|---|
| **I. The Shard Monk** (full health) | Close-range weapons in the courtyard: axe, stone hand, lances, serpent. |
| **II. The Arsenal** (70%) | Moros arms himself: a siege cannon, twin gatlings and a rocket pod circle him. From now on he also fights at range. |
| **III. The Living Temple** (40%) | The titan, then the Stone Roc, the Skybreaker, the Shard Flood and **the Great Dragon**. |

| New weapon | What it does | How to survive |
|---|---|---|
| **Shard Gatlings** | Two six-barrel guns spray stone rounds | Break line of sight behind a building, block, or dash |
| **Siege Cannon** | A banded cannon lobs stone balls onto red circles | Get out of the circle |
| **Rocket Barrage** | A pod fires homing rockets that explode on impact | Run behind buildings: rockets blow up on the walls |
| **The Great Dragon** | A winged stone dragon the size of a street circles you, breathes fire along a red line, then dives | Get off the red line, get out of the circle |

**He fights you wherever you are.** In the courtyard he uses his close weapons. If you run up onto the rooftops he switches to guns, the cannon, rockets and the dragon. Up there the hunting swarms can't see you unless they fly right over you. If you hide **inside a building**, he can't follow you in, so he sends the stones in after you:
- **Shard Gale:** a storm of stones blows through the doorway and shoves you across the room. Then it reverses and drags you out towards him. Hold block and run against the wind.
- **Seeker Swarms:** small buzzing clusters (balls, rings and whirling columns) search the rooms. If one finds you, it swallows you and hauls you out through the door to Moros. Punch them to smash them.

## Kaito's Powers

The three great powers recharge for **one minute** after each use. The ring around the power's icon at the bottom of the screen fills back up, and the icon glows when the power is ready. Wall runs and the Stone Gauntlets can always be used.

| Key | Power | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Space` | **Wall Run / Rooftop Leap** | Sprint at a building and press Space to run straight up the wall and flip onto the roof. Run across the rooftops and press Space to leap to the next roof. Run off an edge and you drop to the street. |
| `1` | **Thunder Palm** | Charges lightning into both palms and slams the ground. The shockwave (about 13 m) shatters every weapon, hunting swarm, seeker and projectile it reaches, and breaks you out of a grab. |
| `2` | **Guardian Spirit** | A giant blue spirit of light, in your own image, rises out of the ground behind you and punches Moros three times: giant against giant. The last punch crumbles the titan. |
| `3` | **Blade Hand Awakening** | Your hands ignite blue and time slows. You flash-step in and tear into a pinned Moros with a cinematic chain (flying knee, hurricane kick, capoeira kick, side kick, uppercut) that sends him flying. |
| `4` | **Stone Gauntlets** | Stones rise from the ground and wrap your fists. Your punches hit 60% harder, a punch that lands on one of Moros's weapons shatters it, and eight punches to the titan's legs bring it down. Press again to drop them. |

Jump straight into the boss fight: `Build\LastStance.exe -boss`

## Features

- ⚔️ **Motion-captured fighters:** realistic human characters animated with real mocap strikes, blocks, knockdowns and get-ups
- 🥊 **Physical hit reactions:** bodies bend away from the exact spot each fist or foot lands
- 🦶 **Planted footwork:** feet grip the floor during strikes instead of sliding
- 🦵 **Support-leg IK:** the kicking leg is released from foot locking while its planted leg remains stable, preventing elastic knees during high kicks, knees and spins
- 💥 **Cinematic impact:** hit-stop, camera shake, red hit splashes, sparks, shockwave rings and slow-motion knockouts
- 🛡️ **Deep defence:** block, parry, dodge, throw and throw break
- 🧠 **Smart AI opponent:** reads your attacks, blocks, parries, sidesteps, punishes mistakes and throws you if you turtle
- 🎥 **Dynamic 2.5D camera:** Tekken-style, orbits as fighters circle, crane-shot intros and slow-motion K.O. orbits
- 🏃 **Parkour:** wall runs up onto the roofs, rooftop leaps, drops and hard landings, all with real mocap
- ⚡ **Hero powers:** Thunder Palm, Guardian Spirit, Blade Hand Awakening and Stone Gauntlets, with recharge rings on the HUD
- 🎬 **Boss-fight camera director:** cuts automatically between side, low-angle, over-the-villain's-shoulder, orbit, chase, front-flee and aerial shots; always keeps Kaito, Moros and his weapon in view and never goes behind a building or tree
- 🪨 **Shard swarm:** thousands of GPU-instanced stone chips that flock, form serpents and tornadoes, and assemble into weapons and a titan
- 🥁 **Taiko battle soundtrack** with punchy impact sound effects
- 🏆 **Full match flow:** round intros with a temple gong, "FIGHT!", K.O., "PERFECT", victory poses, rematch
